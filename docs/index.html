Wonder Pebbles

Wonder Pebbles is a YouTube channel of short, gentle learning cartoons for toddlers and preschoolers (ages 2–5). Pebble, a friendly little blue pebble, teaches numbers and counting, colors and shapes, the ABCs, and simple science about nature.

Videos are made with open-source tools and uploaded to our own channel by an automated publishing tool (the "Wonder Pebbles Uploader"). Every video is reviewed by a person before it is made public.
