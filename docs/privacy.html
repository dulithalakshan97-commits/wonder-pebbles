Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

The Wonder Pebbles Uploader is an internal tool used only by the owner of the Wonder Pebbles YouTube channel to upload videos that the owner created to the owner's own channel.

What data we access

The tool uses the YouTube Data API with the youtube.upload permission, granted by the channel owner. It is used only to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, tags, audience ("made for kids") settings and thumbnails.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information about viewers or other YouTube users.
  • We do not access any other Google account data.
  • We do not use cookies or tracking on this website.

Storage and security

The OAuth credentials are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets and are used only by the automated upload job. The channel owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Our videos are made for children and are marked "made for kids" on YouTube, following YouTube's and COPPA rules. This tool does not collect any information from children.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.
